Who we are

Our mission

The mission of Living Hope Chaplaincy is to ensure hospital patients and their families receive the spiritual care they need.

Our trained volunteers provide encouragement, a listening ear, prayer, and helpful resources to support patients and families on the journey toward healing. We also provide financial assistance to patients who are struggling to pay medical deductibles, co-insurance, and parking expenses.

Pastor Steve Austin launched Living Hope Chaplaincy, a 501(c)(3) non-profit, in April 2021 after completing hospital chaplaincy training at MD Anderson Cancer Center.

For patients and families

Request a visit

Whether the visit is for yourself or for a loved one, we would love to come encourage you and pray with you.

When you reach out, it helps to share your name and phone number, the hospital and room number, and whether the visit is for you or someone you love. You are also welcome to share a prayer request.
