About us

Our mission

The mission of Living Hope Chaplaincy is to ensure hospital patients and their families receive the spiritual care they need.

How we began

Our story

Many hospital patients and their families receive little or no spiritual care when they need it most. Living Hope Chaplaincy was founded to help meet that need.

After ministering to the sick for 25 years at the Texas Medical Center in Houston, the largest medical center in the world, Pastor Steve Austin completed hospital chaplaincy training at MD Anderson Cancer Center and launched Living Hope Chaplaincy in April 2021.

The goal is to have teams of trained volunteers providing world-class spiritual care in major hospitals around the United States and abroad.

For patients and families

What we do

Our trained volunteers offer a caring presence, a listening ear, encouragement, prayer, and printed, video, and audio resources to support patients and families on the journey toward healing.

  • Patient and family visits

    Whether the visit is for you or for someone you love, we come to the bedside as a caring presence, with a listening ear and encouragement.

  • Prayer and support

    We would love to come encourage you and pray with you. You are also welcome to share a prayer request when you reach out.

  • Helpful resources

    We offer printed, video, and audio resources, including books, scriptures, and videos to encourage patients, families, and caregivers.

Our founder

Meet Pastor Steve Austin

Pastor Steve Austin has served in full-time ministry since 2000 after practicing law in Houston for eight years. He has been a pastor and Senior Director at Lakewood Church in Houston since 2003, where he has overseen numerous ministries, Christian education classes, and hundreds of volunteers. He is also an author and speaker.

After ministering to the sick in the largest medical center in the world for 25 years, the Texas Medical Center in Houston, Texas, Steve launched Living Hope Chaplaincy in April 2021 after completing hospital chaplaincy training at MD Anderson Cancer Center. His goal is to have teams of trained volunteers providing world-class spiritual care in major hospitals around the United States and abroad. Pastor Steve has been married for over 27 years and has two daughters.

A registered non-profit

A 501(c)(3) organization

Living Hope Chaplaincy is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Your tax-deductible donation helps us provide spiritual resources and medical benevolence to patients and their families. We also provide financial assistance to patients who are struggling to pay medical deductibles, co-insurance, and parking expenses.
