For hospitals

More spiritual care for your patients, at no cost

Our trained volunteers offer spiritual care to patients, families, and healthcare workers. Our service is free.

Whether a hospital has 10–12 staff chaplains covering 650–1,000 beds or no spiritual care or chaplaincy department at all, every hospital can use more help. Our goal is to help hospitals provide the best possible spiritual care to patients, families, and healthcare workers.

Talk with us about a hospital partnership

Our pledge to the hospitals we partner with

  • We will never proselytize.
  • We will never force religion or our personal beliefs on anyone.
  • We will never promote a church or ministry.
  • We will meet every patient where they are and accept them for who they are.
  • We will abide by all hospital protocols and procedures.
  • We will treat every patient, family member, and staff person with the utmost respect.

How our volunteers are screened and trained

Visiting hospital patients and their families is a sacred trust, so every volunteer is screened three times.

  1. 1

    By their church

    Well-established partner churches pre-screen and recommend prospective volunteers.

  2. 2

    By Living Hope

    A written application and an in-depth interview with Living Hope President Steve Austin. Only the best applicants are selected.

  3. 3

    By the hospital

    Each volunteer completes the assigned hospital's own volunteer screening: background check, health screening, immunizations, and orientation.

Training

Our volunteer training was developed in consultation with board-certified chaplains (BCC) from several leading hospitals and is consistent with content taught in Clinical Pastoral Education (CPE) programs. It includes classroom teaching and role-playing, followed by shadowing a hospital ministry veteran or staff chaplain when available. Volunteers continue to receive ongoing training and supervision after they start.

For churches

Partner with us in this ministry

There is no cost for your church to partner with Living Hope.

According to the American Hospital Association, over 33 million Americans are admitted to the hospital each year. Many patients and their families receive little or no spiritual care when they need it most. Living Hope partners with churches to recruit and train volunteers who provide spiritual care to patients, their families, and healthcare workers.

Talk with us about a church partnership

How Living Hope can benefit your church

Care for your members

Our volunteers can visit your hospitalized members as a supplement to your own pastoral staff and volunteers. We let them know their church asked us to visit, and we keep you updated.

An outlet for your people

Members with a passion for hospital ministry can impact more lives. Their training and hands-on experience also help them care for your own members.

A natural connection

We don't promote any church, but patients and families often ask volunteers where they go to church. That offers an organic way to introduce your church.
