For new volunteers

Volunteer chaplain training

Welcome. We're so glad you're here. These short training videos introduce the heart and the practice of hospital chaplaincy, and prepare you to sit with patients and families when they need it most.

Training videos Certification quiz

How it works

Three simple steps

Go at your own pace. You can pause a video and come back to it any time.

  1. 1

    Watch the videos in order

    Ten videos: a welcome, six modules, and next steps. Each builds on the one before, so find a quiet spot and take notes if that helps you.

  2. 2

    Take the quiz

    Ten questions at the bottom of this page, based on the modules: true or false, multiple choice, and two short written answers.

  3. 3

    Get certified

    Our team reviews your quiz, including your written answers, and follows up with you about next steps.

Certification quiz

Check your understanding

Ten questions based on the modules. Take your time; there are no trick questions.

1True or falseKnowing your “why” can help sustain you through difficult visits.

2Multiple choiceWhy does it matter to reflect on your own “why” before you begin?

3Multiple choiceIn this training, what does “walking in power” look like at the bedside?

4True or falseServing with authority means you may set aside a nurse's instructions if you feel strongly about something.

5Multiple choiceWhich of these best shows you are listening well?

6True or falseIf a patient says they would rather not pray, you respect their wishes and keep offering your caring presence.

7Multiple choiceYou see an isolation or precautions sign on a patient's door. What should you do?

8Multiple choiceAfter a visit, what can you share about the patient outside the hospital?

9Short answerIn your own words, what does listening well at the bedside look like?

10Short answerWhat is your “why” for serving in hospital chaplaincy?

All questions are required. True or false and multiple choice answers are scored right away; written answers are reviewed by our team.
